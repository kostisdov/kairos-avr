# Scoring Rubric — AVR Durability Challenge

**Challenge:** Predictive risk of bioprosthetic valve failure — structural valve deterioration (SVD) and long-term durability after aortic valve replacement (SAVR / TAVR).

Each submission is scored 1–5 on every criterion below. Weighted score = Σ (criterion score × weight).

## 4.1 Scoring Rubric (1–5 per criterion)

| Criterion | Weight | Score 1–5 Descriptors |
|---|---|---|
| **Clinical Validity** | 25% | 1: No clinical grounding<br>2: Minimal relevance to AVR/SVD durability<br>3: Adequate clinical rationale<br>4: Strong evidence-based design<br>5: Exceptional clinical depth & real-world applicability |
| **ML Approach** | 25% | 1: No ML component<br>2: Generic or inappropriate model choice<br>3: Reasonable ML methodology<br>4: Well-justified feature selection & model<br>5: Innovative, rigorous & reproducible ML pipeline |
| **Study Design Quality** | 20% | 1: No structure<br>2: Major gaps in protocol<br>3: Adequate design with minor gaps<br>4: Clear endpoints, cohort & statistical plan<br>5: Publication-ready protocol design |
| **GitHub & Documentation** | 15% | 1: No submission or unreadable<br>2: Incomplete files<br>3: All files present, basic README<br>4: Well-organized, clear README & rationale<br>5: Exemplary documentation, reproducible structure |
| **Presentation** | 15% | 1: Unclear or unprepared<br>2: Partially communicated<br>3: Clear delivery with minor gaps<br>4: Confident, well-structured, good Q&A<br>5: Outstanding clarity, storytelling & team cohesion |
